WHO WE ARE

The Swag Tones are a seven-piece, high-energy dance band blending funk, soul, rock, and a taste of AM Gold into a genre and era fluid live experience.

Formed in 2019 as the house band at the Swag Shop in Boulder Creek, the band features dual lead vocalists, sax, keys, guitar, percussion, and a tight rhythm section delivering dynamic sets that keep audiences moving and guessing what's next.
